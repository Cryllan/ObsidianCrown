Frequently Asked Questions

Further information.

Are you currently hiring?

Recruitment availability is displayed in our application portal. Listed disciplines are areas of interest and do not necessarily represent active vacancies.

What should my portfolio contain?

Include relevant examples of your previous work, demonstrations of your technical or creative abilities, and projects that you have permission to share.

Do I need professional experience?

Experience requirements depend on the needs of individual projects. The quality and relevance of your portfolio are important considerations.

Are opportunities paid?

Compensation, responsibilities, and engagement terms will depend on the specific opportunity and will be discussed before any work begins. A portfolio submission does not establish an employment or contractor agreement.

Can I apply for multiple disciplines?

You may express interest in different disciplines. Duplicate submissions for the same Roblox username and discipline are restricted within a 24-hour period.

How is my information handled?

Our recruitment data practices are described in the Privacy Policy. Please review it before submitting your portfolio.
