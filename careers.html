05 / Frequently Asked Questions

A few answers.

Information for individuals interested in future opportunities at Obsidian Crown.

Is Obsidian Crown currently hiring?

Specific vacancies have not yet been announced. This page introduces the disciplines we are interested in for current and future development.

Do I need a portfolio?

A portfolio or relevant work samples are encouraged, as they help demonstrate your abilities, experience, and creative approach.

Are these paid opportunities?

Compensation and engagement arrangements have not yet been established for the disciplines listed. Any applicable terms will be communicated before an agreement is made.

What projects are you developing?

Our featured project is Throw Meat for Dinos!, a prehistoric dinosaur collection experience currently in development. Additional projects will be announced through our official website.

Can I apply for multiple disciplines?

When submissions open, you may identify your primary discipline and describe additional relevant skills in your introduction.

How will I know when applications open?

Application availability will be announced on this careers page. Please check back for future updates.
