Frequently Asked Questions

Before you
reach out.

How do I apply?

Contact gryph0n directly through Discord with your introduction, relevant experience and portfolio.

Are there currently open positions?

We welcome portfolio introductions and inquiries regarding future opportunities. Availability depends on current project requirements.

Do I need a portfolio?

Relevant examples of your work are strongly encouraged. A portfolio helps us understand your abilities and creative approach.

Can I inquire about an existing application?

Yes. Contact us through Discord and provide your existing application reference if you have one.

Are opportunities paid?

Compensation, responsibilities and engagement terms are discussed individually when a specific opportunity is available.

How is my information handled?

Review our Privacy Policy before sharing information. Discord correspondence is also subject to Discord's own privacy practices. Do not share passwords, payment credentials or sensitive account information.

What about business inquiries?

Business proposals, partnerships and commercial opportunities can be directed to the same Discord account. Visit our Contact page for further information.
