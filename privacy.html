01 / Introduction

Our commitment.

Obsidian Crown is an independent game development studio focused on the creation and design of interactive experiences, including experiences developed for the Roblox platform.

This proposed Privacy Policy describes how information is intended to be handled when individuals visit our official website, communicate with us, or participate in our recruitment and portfolio submission process once it becomes available.

References to "Obsidian Crown," "we," "our," and "us" refer to the operator of the Obsidian Crown website and its associated recruitment services.

This policy applies to our website at obsidiancrown.org and does not replace the privacy policies of Roblox, Discord, or other independent third-party services.

02 / Information We Collect

What we collect.

We intend to limit recruitment information to what is reasonably necessary to evaluate creative and technical experience, manage submissions, and communicate with applicants.

Recruitment Information

When portfolio submissions become available, the recruitment form is designed to request the following information:

Information Purpose
Preferred name Identifying and addressing applicants.
Roblox username Identifying the account associated with an application.
Discord username (optional) Facilitating permitted recruitment communication.
Preferred discipline Identifying relevant areas of creative or technical interest.
Development experience Understanding an applicant's self-reported experience.
Portfolio URL Reviewing relevant examples of previous work.
Written introduction Understanding qualifications and creative interests.
Privacy acknowledgement Recording the policy version acknowledged at submission.

Automatically Generated Records

Our recruitment backend is designed to generate and maintain administrative information associated with applications, including:

  • Unique application reference identifiers.
  • Application creation and update timestamps.
  • Recruitment and application status.
  • Application status history.
  • Reviewer identifiers and relevant administrative notes, where used.

Technical Information

Our hosting and security providers may process technical information associated with website requests, including IP addresses, browser information, device characteristics, request timestamps, and security-related signals.

Our planned Turnstile verification process sends a verification token and, when available, the requesting IP address to Cloudflare for security validation. These values are not included in our recruitment application database schema.

Information you should not submit

Do not include passwords, authentication codes, government identification numbers, financial account details, home addresses, medical information, or other sensitive personal information in your application or portfolio introduction.

03 / Use of Information

Why we use information.

Information submitted through our planned recruitment system may be used for the following purposes:

  • Receiving and organizing expressions of interest.
  • Evaluating portfolios and relevant experience.
  • Identifying potential creative and technical collaborators.
  • Communicating about recruitment and project opportunities.
  • Maintaining recruitment status and administrative records.
  • Preventing spam, abuse, and duplicate submissions.
  • Protecting the integrity and security of our website.
  • Responding to applicable legal obligations.

Where applicable privacy laws require a lawful basis for processing, that basis must be determined according to the purpose and circumstances of the processing. These may include taking steps requested by an applicant before entering an agreement, legitimate interests in operating and securing recruitment services, compliance with legal obligations, or consent where appropriate.

Acknowledging this Privacy Policy does not constitute blanket consent to unrelated uses of personal information.

04 / Storage & Security

Protecting your information.

Our website is hosted using Cloudflare Pages. The recruitment backend is designed to store application records in a Cloudflare D1 database.

The recruitment system incorporates or is intended to incorporate safeguards appropriate to its operation, including:

  • HTTPS connections for website and application requests.
  • Server-side application processing.
  • Prepared database statements and input validation.
  • Request size limits and duplicate submission controls.
  • Server-side Turnstile security verification.
  • Protected storage of backend credentials.
  • Restricted administrative access before recruitment opens.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can guarantee absolute security. We intend to maintain reasonable safeguards and respond appropriately to identified security incidents.

Recruitment access

Public portfolio submissions will remain disabled until appropriate administrative access controls, privacy procedures, and end-to-end security testing are established.

05 / Data Retention

Keeping only what is necessary.

Our proposed retention approach is to maintain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, subject to applicable legal obligations.

Record Type Proposed Retention
Active applications For the duration of the recruitment review, with a maximum review period of 12 months unless justified otherwise.
Declined or withdrawn applications Up to 90 days following the final decision or withdrawal, unless a longer period is legally necessary.
Accepted applications As necessary for onboarding, engagement administration, and applicable legal obligations.
Application history Generally retained alongside its associated application.
Provider backups and technical logs Subject to provider settings, service retention periods, and applicable obligations.

Information may need to be retained for longer where reasonably necessary to resolve disputes, investigate security incidents, or comply with applicable law.

Proposed retention schedule

These retention periods have not yet been adopted or implemented through automated deletion procedures. Recruitment will remain closed until the studio establishes an appropriate retention and deletion process.

07 / Cookies & Technology

Website technology.

The website pages developed for Obsidian Crown do not currently include dedicated advertising trackers or marketing analytics scripts in their supplied source code.

Our infrastructure providers may use cookies, browser storage, or similar technologies for security, service delivery, abuse prevention, and other operational purposes.

Cloudflare Turnstile

Turnstile is intended to help distinguish legitimate visitors from automated or abusive requests. It may process browser and device signals to perform security verification.

Additional information is available in Cloudflare's Turnstile Privacy Addendum.

If nonessential analytics, advertising, or similar tracking technologies are introduced in the future, this policy and any applicable consent mechanisms should be updated before their use.

08 / Information Sharing

Responsible disclosure.

Our proposed recruitment practices do not include selling applicant information or sharing it for targeted advertising.

Information may be disclosed where reasonably necessary to:

  • Authorized recruitment personnel and relevant studio decision-makers.
  • Service providers supporting hosting, security, storage, and communication.
  • Professional advisers where necessary and appropriate.
  • Authorities or other parties when disclosure is required by applicable law.
  • Relevant parties where necessary to protect rights, safety, or security.

Access to recruitment information should be limited to individuals who require that information for legitimate recruitment or administrative purposes.

09 / Your Privacy Rights

Your information. Your rights.

Depending on your location and applicable privacy laws, you may have rights concerning the personal information associated with you.

These rights may include the ability to:

  • Request access to personal information held about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of personal information, subject to applicable exceptions.
  • Withdraw a recruitment application.
  • Object to or request restriction of certain processing activities.
  • Request a copy of information in a portable format where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.
  • Submit a complaint to an applicable privacy or data protection authority.

Requests may require reasonable verification to establish that the requester is entitled to access or modify the information concerned. An application reference identifier alone may not be sufficient to establish identity.

Applicable rights, response periods, and exceptions depend on the relevant jurisdiction and circumstances.

10 / Children's Privacy

Protecting younger visitors.

We recognize that the Roblox platform includes younger audiences and that additional privacy considerations apply when processing children's information.

Our proposed recruitment system is intended for individuals aged 18 years or older. It is not intended to collect recruitment applications from children or other minors.

The recruitment form must incorporate appropriate eligibility controls before submissions are enabled. We do not intend to request dates of birth or government identification documents through the public application form.

If we become aware that personal information has been collected from an ineligible minor, we intend to take appropriate steps to remove that information, subject to applicable legal requirements.

This proposed recruitment eligibility restriction does not determine eligibility to play our Roblox experiences, which are subject to applicable platform requirements.

11 / International Processing

A connected world.

Our website and technology providers may operate infrastructure in multiple countries. Information may therefore be processed or accessed outside the country in which a visitor or applicant resides.

Where applicable laws impose requirements on international transfers of personal information, appropriate transfer safeguards must be established before the relevant processing takes place.

This policy does not represent a guarantee that all information remains within a particular country or geographic region.

12 / Policy Changes

Continued transparency.

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Obsidian Crown develops its website, introduces new services, changes its recruitment procedures, or responds to applicable legal requirements.

When the policy is formally adopted or subsequently revised, its version number and effective date should identify the applicable document.

Material changes should be communicated as appropriate. Where required, renewed acknowledgement or consent should be obtained before affected processing occurs.
