Professional Correspondence

Before reaching out.

Introduce yourself.

Include your name or professional alias, your area of expertise and a brief explanation of why you are contacting Obsidian Crown.

Provide relevant information.

For business inquiries, outline the opportunity you wish to discuss. For development and career inquiries, include relevant experience and a link to your portfolio where available.

Maintain professionalism.

We appreciate clear, respectful and purposeful communication. Please allow sufficient time for a response and avoid sending repeated messages. Never share passwords or other sensitive account information.
